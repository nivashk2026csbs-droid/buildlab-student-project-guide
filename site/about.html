ABOUT BUILDLAB

Good projects begin with good questions.

BuildLab is a simple learning resource for students who want to turn an idea into a thoughtful, practical project.

Our approach

Start with a real need. Make a small version. Learn from feedback. This approach helps keep a project achievable while leaving space to explore new skills.

The project ideas on this site are starting points. Adapt them to your course requirements, available materials, and the people who might use your solution.

A GOOD PROJECT IS
  • Clear about the problem
  • Designed for a real user
  • Built in manageable steps
  • Honest about what it can do
