A SIMPLE PROCESS

Build it one clear step at a time.

You do not need to know everything before you begin. Move through these stages, ask for feedback, and keep improving your idea.

  1. 01

    Describe the problem

    Write down who experiences it, when it happens, and why it matters. Keep your first version specific.

  2. 02

    Set a project goal

    Say what your project will do. List what is in scope and what can wait for a later version.

  3. 03

    Plan the structure

    Sketch the pages, components, or system flow. Choose tools that match your course and available resources.

  4. 04

    Make a small prototype

    Build the simplest working version. Check each part as you go and keep a note of decisions.

  5. 05

    Review and present

    Ask someone to try it, make a useful improvement, and prepare to explain your choices and learning.

Keep a project log

Record what you tried, what worked, what did not, and what you will do next. It makes your progress easier to understand and present.
